# Main

## Profile

I have taught skiing for more than 25 years and diving for over 15, on four continents, to every level and age. I grew up and still live in the mountains around Laax, and I spend as much time outside as I can: skiing, ski touring, climbing, flying, biking, running and diving. My day job is running a software company, which has made me good at organising people, staying calm when plans change and explaining things clearly in five languages.

## Sport experience

- **Ski instructor trainer**, Swiss Ski School Laax · since 2000
- **Independent dive instructor**, ticinodive.ch · 2006 – 2022
- Ski and freeride teaching and guiding at every level and age
- Six years on the ski demo team
- Salomon ski promoter and mountaineering ambassador, Cube Bikes factory co-pilot
- Founder of 27summits.ch, a human-powered project going from the lowest to the highest point of each Swiss canton in one push (bike, run, ski touring, mountaineering, climbing, cross-country skiing); 26 of 27 done
