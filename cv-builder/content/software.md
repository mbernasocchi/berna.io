# Main

## Profile

Geographer and software engineer who has worked in open-source GIS since 2007. I created QField, the field app for QGIS, which has passed two million downloads, and I run OPENGIS.ch, a company of around 50 people and the second biggest contributor to QGIS core. Since 2020 I have chaired QGIS.org, and since 2022 I have sat on the board of the Open Source Geospatial Foundation. My work moves between code, product, company and community governance, and I am happiest when those four pull in the same direction.

## Experience

### OPENGIS.ch, Laax
*CEO since Dec 2014 · Founder since Dec 2010*

Founded as a sole proprietorship and incorporated as OPENGIS.ch GmbH in December 2014, co-owned with Matthias Kuhn. Grew the company from a one-person business to around 50 people working on QGIS core, QField, QFieldCloud and Georama, with support contracts and custom development for public administrations, NGOs and companies across Europe and beyond.

- Lead strategy, sales and operations; OPENGIS.ch is the second biggest contributor to QGIS core
- Launched a QGIS sustainability initiative in 2019, funded through support contracts
- Launched Georama (georama.io) in 2026 for publishing maps and geospatial stories on the web

### QField and QFieldCloud
*Creator and product lead · 2011 – present*

- Ported QGIS (over a million lines of code) and its 10+ C/C++ dependency libraries to Android on my own, starting as a Google Summer of Code project in 2011; this became QField
- QField has over two million downloads, about 1.7 million installs on Google Play and around half a million monthly active users on Android
- Project manager and co-architect of QFieldCloud, including the backend data model and Docker infrastructure; the cloud service now funds most of QField's development
- QField won Best of Swiss Apps in 2022

### GEM Foundation (Global Earthquake Model)
*QGIS and Python consultant · Mar 2013 – Oct 2020*

Consultant, developer and trainer on the OpenQuake Integrated Risk Modelling Toolkit (PyQt with JavaScript), working on the more complex parts of the user-facing tools and the database models.

### The World Bank and AusAID (InaSAFE)
*Geospatial consultant, Python developer and open-source advocate · Aug 2012 – Jul 2017*

Co-developer of InaSAFE, open software for natural hazard impact scenarios. Led the data aggregation, post-processing and metadata modules. InaSAFE won a Black Duck Open Source Rookies of the Year award.
