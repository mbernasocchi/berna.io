# Main

## Profile

Geographer, software engineer and entrepreneur. I created QField, the field app for QGIS with over two million downloads, and I run OPENGIS.ch, a company of around 50 people and the second biggest contributor to QGIS core. I chair QGIS.org and sit on the OSGeo board. Outside work I am a ski instructor trainer, a technical diving instructor and a paraglider pilot.

## Current roles

- **CEO**, OPENGIS.ch · since Dec 2014 (founder since Dec 2010)
- **Creator and product lead**, QField and QFieldCloud · since 2011
- **Chair**, QGIS.org · since May 2020 (Co-chair 2018 – 2020)
- **Board member**, Open Source Geospatial Foundation (OSGeo) · since Jan 2022
- **Ski instructor trainer**, Swiss Ski School Laax · since 2000

## Previous experience

- QGIS and Python consultant, GEM Foundation · 2013 – 2020
- Geospatial consultant and developer (InaSAFE), The World Bank and AusAID · 2012 – 2017
- Google Summer of Code mentor (2012) and student developer, QGIS for Android (2011)
- Web development teacher, SAE Institute Zurich · 2010 – 2015
- Founder and lead developer, bernawebdesign.ch · 2002 – 2010
- Independent dive instructor, ticinodive.ch · 2006 – 2022; seasons as ski and dive instructor in New Zealand, Colorado, Mexico, Curaçao and Malaysia · 2002 – 2010
- Night nursing assistant, University Hospital Zurich · 2003 – 2006

## Highlights

- Ported QGIS and its 10+ C/C++ dependencies to Android on my own; this became QField, Best of Swiss Apps 2022
- Opening keynote at FOSS4G 2023 (Prizren) and *Who pays your bills?* at FOSS4G 2025
- Co-author of *The QGIS project: Spatial without compromise*, Patterns, 2025
- Organiser of the QGIS User Conference 2026 in Laax, 420 people from more than 30 countries
- 27summits.ch: lowest to highest point of 26 Swiss cantons under my own power, one to go
